# Inductive non-fiction Bibliography

- Arendt, Hannah. *Eichmann in Jerusalem: A Report on the Banality of Evil* (Harmondsworth: Penguin Books, 1976; first edn 1963)

- Carson, Rachel. *Silent Spring* (Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 2002; first edn 1962).

- Cooper, Anna Julia. *A Voice from the South: By A Black Woman of the South* (Portland: Mint Editions, 2021; first edn 1892).

- De Beauvoir, Simone. *The Ethics of Ambiguity*, trans. Bernard Frechtman (New York: Open Road Integrated Media, 2018; first published in French in 1947)

    *The Second Sex*, trans. Constance Borde and Sheila Malovany-Chevallier (London: Vintage, 2011; first published in French in 1949).

- Doubrovsky, Serge. *Fils* (Paris: Galilée, 1977).

- Eaglestone, Robert. *Ethical Criticism: Reading after Levinas* (Edinburgh: Edinburgh University Press, 2008)

- hooks, bell. *Ain’t I a Woman: Black Women and Feminism* (New York: Routledge, 2015; first edn 1981).

- Lazar, David (ed.). *Truth in Nonfiction: Essays* (Iowa City: University of Iowa Press, 2008)

- Paul, L. A. *Transformative Experience* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2014).

For a translation, see Michael Mascuch, Rudolf Dekker, and Arianne Baggerman, 'Egodocuments and History: A Short Account of the Longue Durée', *The Historian*, Vol. 78, No. 1 (Spring 2016), pp. 11-56.
